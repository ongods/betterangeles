# Accessibility

Ways to navigate this guide and report access barriers.

Use the “Skip to content” link at the start of each page to bypass navigation.
Search and filters can be used with a keyboard, and labels identify their purpose.
The city directory is a text list and does not require a map or location access.

You can enlarge text using your browser’s zoom controls. Pages adapt to narrow
screens. Sources and archived PDFs are linked from the relevant guides; some
source PDFs may have accessibility limitations.

This is an evolving community guide. No completed independent accessibility
audit or conformance certification is claimed.

If a page prevents you from finding information, use
[Contact & Help](/contact) to report the page address, what you were trying to do,
and the barrier you encountered. Avoid posting private transaction information.
