# Contact & Help

Find official transaction destinations and report a correction to BetterAngeles.

## Government transactions

BetterAngeles is a community information guide. Government offices handle
applications, payments, appointments, and decisions through their own systems.

- [Business permit applications, renewal, and tracking: eBPLS](https://cityofangeles.ph/bpls/).
- [Health ID registration and appointments](https://cityofangeles.ph/healthcard/).
- [Angeles City Government website](https://angelescity.gov.ph/).
- [Available office, barangay, and school records](/directory).
- [City Hall schedule advice](/government/departments/office-schedules).
- [Available service guides](/services).

Confirm current requirements and office hours before visiting. Some guide
details come from archived charters, and the directory is incomplete. For urgent
assistance, use the emergency services for your location; this website does not
receive emergency reports.

## Correct information in this guide

[Open an issue in the BetterAngeles project](https://github.com/ongods/betterangeles/issues/new)
to report a broken link, outdated detail, or missing source. A GitHub account is
required. You can also read the
[public project repository](https://github.com/ongods/betterangeles).

Include the page address, the detail needing correction, and a dated source if
available. Do not include identity documents, medical records, passwords, or
private application details in a public issue. For a transaction concern,
contact the responsible office through its official system.

## About the information

The guide combines saved city documents with a source extraction collected on
28 October 2026. Population counts retain their census year; school records
retain their source year; reported costs and events retain their original dates.
A source link does not establish complete coverage or current availability.

New information can be added without replacing the saved evidence. Map
boundaries, verified jeepney paths, and current project progress have not yet been
obtained. They are not estimated from names or older announcements.
