# City Hall office schedules — Angeles City

Check the city government workweek and the dated October 2026 office schedule advisory.

## Advisory published October 15, 2026

The city's adjusted-work-schedule announcement says the city government uses a **Monday–Thursday compressed workweek**, with Friday as a non-working day. For **October 19–22, 2026**, it specifies **regular eight-hour workdays**, because **Friday, October 23** is a special non-working day in Angeles City.

The announcement does not specify clock-in, opening, or closing times. Confirm transaction hours with the office before visiting. Essential and 24/7 frontline services have separate staffing arrangements; do not apply this City Hall schedule to hospital or emergency-service availability.

Some service pages retain Monday–Friday hours printed in the August 2026 charter copies. Those are historical source details. This newer advisory should be checked when planning a city government transaction.

For dates after this advisory's October 19–22 period, consult the [latest city announcements](https://angelescity.gov.ph/news/) for the applicable hours and holiday arrangements.
