CITY MAYOR
Mayor Carmelo “Jon” B. Lazatin II
Mayor Carmelo “Jon” B. Lazatin II is the 35th Mayor of Angeles City and the leader of an administration committed to building a progressive, inclusive, resilient, and sustainable city for every Angeleño. His leadership is anchored on responsive governance, meaningful public service, and development that creates opportunities while protecting the city’s people, environment, heritage, and future.
Under Mayor Jon’s leadership, Angeles City continues to pursue a clear development direction—a Maunlad na Angeles para sa lahat. His administration works to ensure that the city’s growth is translated into better public services, stronger communities, greater livelihood and employment opportunities, improved access to essential services, safer neighborhoods, and a higher quality of life for its residents.
Mayor Jon’s leadership reflects a people-centered approach to governance, recognizing that the progress of Angeles City is ultimately measured by the well-being and opportunities of its people. From strengthening the local economy and investing in education and healthcare, to advancing public safety, agriculture, tourism, environmental protection, and community development, his administration continues to implement programs that respond to both the present needs and long-term aspirations of Angeleños.
With Angeles City continuing to grow as a center of commerce, culture, tourism, education, and innovation in Central Luzon, Mayor Jon remains focused on sustaining this progress while preserving the city’s distinct identity and heritage. His vision is to build a city that is modern and competitive, yet deeply connected to its communities; economically dynamic, yet inclusive; and progressive, while remaining responsible stewards of its natural and cultural resources.
Good Governance and People-Centered Public Service
Good governance is at the core of Mayor Jon’s leadership. His administration is committed to ensuring that the government remains responsive to the people, accountable for its actions, efficient in delivering services, and transparent in the use of public resources.
Mayor Jon promotes a culture of transparency, accountability, integrity, efficiency, and inclusivity, recognizing that effective governance is measured by the quality of services delivered and the tangible improvements experienced by residents.
His administration continues to strengthen systems and processes that make government services more accessible, convenient, and responsive. It places importance on listening to the concerns of Angeleños, addressing community needs, improving service delivery, and ensuring that programs are implemented with clear purpose and measurable results.
Good governance also means building strong partnerships. Mayor Jon encourages collaboration among communities, barangays, government institutions, private organizations, civic groups, and other stakeholders to address challenges and pursue opportunities for the city.
Under his leadership, governance is not simply about managing the city—it is about building trust, responding to people, creating opportunities, and delivering results. Every program and development initiative is anchored on the broader goal of creating a government that works for and with the people of Angeles City.
Responsive and Inclusive Governance
At the heart of Mayor Jon’s leadership is a commitment to responsive and people-centered governance. His administration seeks to make public service more accessible, efficient, and responsive to the everyday needs of Angeleños.
Mayor Jon promotes a governance approach that values transparency, accountability, efficiency, and inclusivity, while encouraging active participation and collaboration among communities and stakeholders. Programs are designed to ensure that government services and opportunities reach residents across different sectors and communities.
Under his leadership, development is viewed not only through physical improvements and economic growth, but also through the ability of the government to provide meaningful services, respond to concerns, empower communities, and improve the quality of life of its people.
Economic Development, Employment, and Livelihood
Mayor Jon recognizes that sustainable development must create opportunities for Angeleños to earn, build businesses, and achieve greater economic security.
His administration continues to promote local economic growth by supporting businesses, micro, small, and medium enterprises, tourism-related industries, local entrepreneurs, workers, and aspiring business owners. Employment and livelihood initiatives are geared toward connecting residents with job opportunities while providing skills and training that can lead to sustainable sources of income.
The city likewise continues to encourage entrepreneurship and livelihood development through training in areas such as food processing, home-based businesses, beauty and personal care services, events management, creative industries, and other service-based enterprises.
Through these efforts, Mayor Jon seeks to create an environment where economic progress is shared more broadly and where Angeleños are equipped with the skills and opportunities needed to participate in the city’s growth.
Education and Youth Development
Education remains an important foundation of Mayor Jon’s development agenda.
His administration continues to support initiatives that provide Kabataang Angeleños with access to quality education, technical and vocational skills, professional development, and opportunities for employment. The city recognizes that investing in the youth is an investment in the long-term future of Angeles City.
The City College of Angeles (CCA) has continued to strengthen its academic programs, industry partnerships, skills training, internships, and employment opportunities, helping prepare students for both local and global opportunities.
Beyond formal education, the administration supports programs that encourage youth participation, leadership, creativity, innovation, and community involvement. Mayor Jon believes that young Angeleños should not only be prepared to enter the workforce but also empowered to become active contributors to the development of their city.
Public Health and Social Protection
Improving access to healthcare and social assistance remains a key priority of Mayor Jon’s administration.
Through programs such as the Lingap Lazatin Program, Mayor Jon provides assistance for diagnostic procedures, laboratory services, hospital expenses, and medicines to qualified beneficiaries. These initiatives aim to help families manage the financial burden of healthcare and ensure that medical assistance is accessible to those who need it.
The administration likewise continues to strengthen community-based health programs and social protection initiatives for senior citizens, persons with disabilities, families in difficult circumstances, children, youth, and other vulnerable sectors.
For Mayor Jon, public service extends beyond providing assistance during times of need. It also means building systems and programs that promote dignity, well-being, and a better quality of life for every Angeleño.
Peace, Order, and Public Safety
A safe and secure community is essential to sustainable development.
Mayor Jon continues to strengthen initiatives aimed at improving public safety through modern technology, improved monitoring capabilities, community-based interventions, and closer coordination among stakeholders.
The city has expanded its use of technology in public safety, including a network of high-definition CCTV cameras, GPS monitoring for patrol vehicles, command and monitoring facilities, and other measures designed to improve incident response and strengthen crime prevention.
The administration also recognizes that maintaining peace and order requires more than law enforcement. It involves addressing the social conditions that contribute to vulnerability, strengthening community participation, and providing appropriate interventions for individuals and families at risk.
Rehabilitation, Recovery, and Second Chances
Mayor Jon’s approach to public safety also emphasizes rehabilitation and community reintegration.
Through strengthened anti-drug and rehabilitation initiatives, the City Government continues to provide individuals with opportunities to recover, rebuild their lives, and return productively to their families and communities.
The Balay Silangan Reformation Center has been developed into a more holistic rehabilitation environment, incorporating counseling, character formation, aftercare, livelihood training, agriculture and hydroponics, first aid and emergency response, and other activities that support personal development.
These efforts reflect Mayor Jon’s belief that safer communities are built not only through prevention and enforcement, but also by providing people with opportunities for recovery, livelihood, dignity, and a meaningful second chance.
Agriculture, Food Security, and Livelihood
Mayor Jon recognizes agriculture as an important component of food security, economic resilience, and community livelihood.
The City Government continues to support local farmers through programs involving rice production, high-value crops, coffee production, vegetable gardening, livestock, fisheries, organic agriculture, and other sustainable agricultural activities.
One of the administration’s notable initiatives is the Local Harvest – Direct Purchase of Palay Program, which was implemented to help protect local farmers during a period of exceptionally low palay prices. When private traders were offering approximately ₱9 to ₱11 per kilogram, the City Government directly purchased palay from local farmers at ₱17 per kilogram, providing a more favorable price for their harvest.
More than 69,000 kilograms of palay were purchased from 18 farmers covering more than 20 hectares of farmland, with more than ₱1.177 million directly paid to local farmers. The program helped increase farmers’ income by as much as 47 percent during the period.
The administration also continues to promote organic agriculture, coffee production, livestock and fisheries development, and agricultural livelihood programs, while providing farmers with technical assistance, equipment, training, and market opportunities.
Tourism, Culture, Heritage, and Creative Industries
Tourism is another important pillar of Mayor Jon’s vision for a thriving Angeles City.
His administration continues to promote the city’s distinct Kapampangan identity, cultural heritage, culinary traditions, religious heritage, arts, festivals, and creative industries while developing new experiences that encourage both residents and visitors to discover Angeles City.
Programs such as Pamanang Lakad and the revitalization of the Museo ning Angeles Heritage Area provide platforms for local cuisine, MSMEs, artisans, musicians, cultural performers, and creative entrepreneurs. Through heritage walks, food experiences, cultural presentations, live performances, and community markets, tourism becomes an avenue not only for attracting visitors but also for generating livelihood opportunities for local communities.
The historic proclamation of Pisamban Maragul (Holy Rosary Parish Church) as a Minor Basilica further highlights the city’s significance as a destination for religious, cultural, and heritage tourism.
The city also continues to showcase its traditions on wider platforms, including the Lubenas ning Pasko, which highlights Kapampangan faith, craftsmanship, artistry, and Angeleño pride.
Under Mayor Jon’s leadership, tourism development is pursued with the goal of strengthening the local economy while preserving the identity and heritage that make Angeles City unique.
Environmental Stewardship and Climate Resilience
Environmental protection remains one of Mayor Jon’s key priorities.
Among the administration’s major environmental efforts is the continued rehabilitation and protection of the Abacan River, one of Angeles City’s most significant natural resources. Through intensified clean-up activities, environmental programs, community participation, and inter-agency cooperation, the city continues to promote the protection of its waterways and surrounding communities.
Mayor Jon’s environmental agenda also recognizes the importance of sustainable practices in agriculture, urban development, waste management, and community activities. The administration continues to encourage greater environmental awareness and responsible use of natural resources.
These efforts form part of a broader commitment to building a city that is not only economically progressive but also environmentally responsible and resilient for future generations.
Infrastructure and Urban Development
Mayor Jon’s administration also recognizes the importance of infrastructure and urban development in supporting a growing city.
Development efforts focus on improving public facilities, strengthening essential services, enhancing mobility and accessibility, and creating spaces that contribute to safer, more livable, and more sustainable communities.
Urban development under his leadership is pursued alongside environmental protection and community needs, with the goal of ensuring that the continued growth of Angeles City remains inclusive, organized, and responsive to its residents.
Strengthening Angeles City’s Identity and Future
Beyond individual programs and projects, Mayor Jon’s leadership seeks to strengthen Angeles City’s identity as a city that is progressive yet deeply rooted in its heritage, economically dynamic yet inclusive, and modern while remaining environmentally responsible.
His administration continues to build on the city’s strengths in tourism, culture, education, commerce, agriculture, public service, and community development while creating new opportunities for residents.
Mayor Jon believes that the progress of Angeles City is ultimately measured by the lives of its people—the opportunities available to them, the quality of services they receive, the safety of their communities, and their ability to look toward the future with confidence.
With this vision, Mayor Carmelo “Jon” B. Lazatin II remains committed to advancing a Maunlad na Angeles para sa lahat—a city where development is meaningful, opportunities are accessible, communities are empowered, and every Angeleño has the chance to build a better and more secure future.
