Please be advised of the scheduled power interruption on:
OCTOBER 18, 2026 (SUNDAY)
6:00 AM – 10:00 AM
The National Grid Corporation of the Philippines (NGCP) will conduct technical activities along the Mexico–Angeles 69kV Line 1.
Residents and establishments within the affected areas are advised to make the necessary preparations and plan accordingly during the scheduled interruption.
Please consider all electrical lines and equipment energized at all times and exercise caution.
Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
