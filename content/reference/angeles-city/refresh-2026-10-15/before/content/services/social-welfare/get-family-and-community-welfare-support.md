# Get family and community welfare support — Angeles City

Request an Angeles City social case study report or referral letter for assistance from another institution.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), **City Hall Compound**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## What the service covers

The charter covers individuals and families needing assistance from other institutions. It names PCSO, Angeles University Foundation (AUF), Jose B. Lingad Medical Memorial (JBL), the Public Attorney’s Office (PAO), and other agencies among referral recipients. A report or referral does not itself guarantee acceptance or funding by the receiving institution.

## Requirements

**For medical assistance referrals:** personal request letter; medical certificate or latest medical abstract; updated hospital bill or latest doctor’s prescription with the amount indicated and stamped by the hospital pharmacy; barangay certificate of indigency; valid ID.

**For PAO referrals:** barangay certificate of indigency and valid ID.

## Steps

1. Explain the need during the intake interview.
2. Submit the applicable documents and complete the eligibility assessment.
3. Arrange the home visit for information validation as listed in the charter.
4. Keep the claim slip and collect the prepared social case study report or referral letter on the advised date.
5. Present the report to the receiving institution and ask about its own requirements.

The charter lists **no fee** and **within five days** under normal circumstances. For city financial assistance, see [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance). Couples preparing to marry can also use [pre-marriage counseling](/services/social-welfare/attend-pre-marriage-counseling).
