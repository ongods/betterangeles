# Register a death certificate — Angeles City

Prepare the Angeles City on-time or delayed death registration documents and related permit requests.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

Local Civil Registry, **2nd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## On-time registration

Submit the **completed Death Certificate signed by the physician attesting the cause of death and the funeral provider’s embalmer**. The charter names the attending physician, nearest relative, person with knowledge of the death, or funeral provider as reporting parties.

The saved document states that death should be registered **within 48 hours** and also **within 30 days after death**. Ask the registrar and funeral provider to clarify these two registration references and the deadline that applies to your case.

| Related permit | Published fee |
| -------------- | ------------- |
| Burial         | ₱50           |
| Disinterment   | ₱75           |
| Transfer       | ₱100          |
| Cremation      | ₱500          |

The charter estimates **20 minutes** for the on-time transaction. Pay the amount in the registry’s order of payment, then present the receipt for signature, registry numbering, and release.

## Delayed registration

The charter lists a **completed and signed Death Certificate**. For a death less than a year earlier, it additionally lists an **official receipt of certification from the funeral parlor** and a residence certificate/cedula. For more than a year earlier, it also lists a **PSA Certificate of No Registration**. Confirm the funeral-parlor document and the checklist for a death exactly one year earlier with the registrar.

Published fee: **₱250**. Published timing: **25 minutes processing, 10 days posting, and five minutes releasing**. Submit the requirements for interview, pay the assessed fee, return with the receipt, and collect the document after the posting period.

For help with funeral expenses, see [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance).
