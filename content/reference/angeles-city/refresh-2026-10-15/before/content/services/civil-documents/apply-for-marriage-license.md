# Apply for a marriage license — Angeles City

Prepare the documents and seminar requirements listed by the Angeles City Local Civil Registry.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

Local Civil Registry, **2nd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Published requirements

The saved charter covers unmarried applicants **18 years old or above**, with **at least one partner residing in Angeles City**. It lists:

- Certificate of Live Birth.
- PSA Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR / CRS Form 4).
- Attendance at the responsible parenthood seminar.
- For a previously married applicant: registered annulment decree or deceased spouse’s death certificate, as applicable.
- For a foreign applicant: certificate of legal capacity to marry issued by the embassy or divorce decree, as applicable. Ask the registrar which evidence is accepted.
- Ages 18–20: parental consent and pre-marriage counseling attendance.
- Ages 21–24: parental advice and pre-marriage counseling attendance.

## Steps, fee, and release

Submit the requirements for review and interview. Pay **₱250** after receiving an order of payment, then present the official receipt. Staff post the application for **10 days** and direct you to the City Health Office for seminar arrangements. Return with the attendance certificates to claim the license.

The charter lists **30 minutes initial processing**, a **10-day posting period**, and **five minutes releasing**. It states the license is valid for **120 days from issuance**. Confirm the current checklist with the registrar before booking the ceremony.

See [pre-marriage counseling registration](/services/social-welfare/attend-pre-marriage-counseling).
