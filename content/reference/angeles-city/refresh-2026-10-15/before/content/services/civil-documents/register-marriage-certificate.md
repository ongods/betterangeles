# Register a marriage certificate — Angeles City

Find the published requirements for on-time or delayed marriage certificate registration in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

Local Civil Registry, **2nd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## On-time registration

The charter says marriage should be registered **within 15 days after solemnization** by the solemnizing officer, either spouse, or parents. Submit the **completed Marriage Certificate signed by the solemnizing officer (MF 102)**. Staff verify it, obtain the registrar’s signature, assign a registry number, and release the registered document.

Published fee: **none**. Published processing estimate: **10 minutes**.

## Delayed registration

Submit a completed **Marriage Certificate (MF 102)**. The saved checklist additionally lists:

- Less than one year after marriage: four copies of the unregistered marriage contract, PSA CENOMAR, and a marriage license or applicable affidavit of cohabitation invoking Article 34 of the Family Code.
- More than one year after marriage: PSA CENOMAR, PSA Certificate of No Registration, and the informant’s residence certificate/cedula.

Ask the registrar to confirm the complete checklist for your case, including a marriage exactly one year earlier. Submit the documents for review and interview, pay after receiving the order of payment, and return with the official receipt. Collection follows the required posting period.

Published fee: **₱250**. Published timing: **25 minutes processing, 10 days posting, and five minutes releasing**.

If you have not yet married, see [marriage license applications](/services/civil-documents/apply-for-marriage-license).
