# Apply for worker health certificate cards — Angeles City

Prepare requirements for white, green, or pink health cards for workers in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Which card applies?

| Card  | Workers covered                                                                                      | Published fee | Published processing estimate |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| White | Food industry and general services, including office personnel, clerks, drivers, and utility workers | ₱200          | 25 minutes                    |
| Green | Manufacturing and commercial industry                                                                | ₱200          | 25 minutes                    |
| Pink  | Entertainment and nocturnal industry                                                                 | ₱300          | 50 minutes                    |

## Requirements

- Birth certificate and residence certificate.
- X-ray result and stool and urine examination results, taken within six months before application.
- Two recent 1×1 ID photographs.
- Food sanitation orientation seminar certification for applicants in food establishments.
- Notarized parental consent for minor applicants for white or green cards.
- For pink cards: previous health certificate and Gram-staining smear result. The charter states that minors cannot obtain a pink card.

## Where to apply and steps

White and green cards: **City Health Office, 3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Pink cards: **Reproductive Health & Wellness Center (RHWC), Surla Street, Balibago, Angeles City**. Published hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break.

1. Submit the requirements for review.
2. Once the laboratory results and documents are approved, obtain an order of payment.
3. Pay at the City Treasurer’s Office for white or green cards, or at the RHWC cashier for pink cards.
4. Present the official receipt to the processing clerk. Pink-card applicants also undergo the RHWC smear-test procedure and assessment; adverse findings are referred for treatment.
5. Receive the approved health certificate or card.
