# Get free check-ups and vaccines, and ask about medicines — Angeles City

Access the consultation and immunization services listed in Angeles City’s Citizen’s Charter.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Medical consultations

The charter lists consultations at all Rural Health Units (RHUs) and Barangay Health Stations (BHSs), Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break. Anybody may use the service. **No documentary requirements or consultation fee** are listed; the published processing estimate is **35 minutes**.

1. Register with the barangay health worker or rural health midwife, who creates your initial treatment record.
2. Have your vital signs taken and provide your medical history.
3. Undergo the health worker’s assessment. Patients with complications are referred to a public health nurse or rural health physician.

## Immunization

The saved charter lists free immunization at all RHUs on **Wednesdays, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break**, for infants aged 0–12 months and pregnant mothers in their second trimester. Bring the infant’s **Early Child Care and Development (ECCD) card** or the mother’s home-based maternal record. New patients provide personal information and receive a record from staff. The published estimate is **25 minutes**.

Health staff check the record, take vital signs, and administer the appropriate vaccine. Ask the RHU to confirm the current vaccination schedule and next visit. See [maternal care and child immunization](/services/health-services/access-maternal-care-and-child-immunization).

## Medicines

Ask the attending health worker whether prescribed medicines are available and what documents are needed to collect them. The saved consultation and immunization charters do not specify a medicine inventory, dispensing schedule, or entitlement to free medicines. For help paying medical costs, see [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance).
