# Get a Community Tax Certificate or cedula — Angeles City

Use the published Angeles City data-slip procedure to request a cedula.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

City Treasurer’s Office, **ground floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Eligibility and requirements

The saved charter covers Angeles City residents aged **18 or above**, employed or unemployed, and businesses or organizations operating in the city. Individuals complete a **data slip**, available at the Taxpayer’s Lounge or Public Assistance and Complaints Desk. Businesses provide the **completed data slip and approved gross receipt** listed in the charter.

## Steps and assessment

Obtain and complete the slip, submit it at the Treasurer’s community-tax desk, and wait for the tax computation. Sign the certificate, pay the assessed tax, and collect the cedula.

The saved fee table lists **₱5 basic tax for individuals** and **₱500 basic tax for corporations**, with additional tax and caps. Its short table does not clearly explain every computation basis, so ask the collection officer to calculate the applicable total from your declared information.

Published estimate: **eight minutes for unemployed applicants**, **10 minutes for employed applicants**. The cedula is distinct from a certified true copy of a property tax declaration.
