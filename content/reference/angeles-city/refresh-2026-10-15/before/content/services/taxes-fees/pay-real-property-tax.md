# Pay real property tax — Angeles City

Obtain a property tax order of payment and pay the assessed amount through the Angeles City Treasurer.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

City Assessor’s Office, **ground floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Obtain the order of payment

The City Assessor’s charter covers taxpayers, property owners, or authorized representatives. Bring a **previous property tax receipt**. If the receipt or tax declaration is unavailable, provide the **owner’s name and property location** so staff can locate the record. Submit at the Assessor’s desk for database checking, verification, and issuance of the **Real Property Tax Order of Payment (RPTOP)**.

The RPTOP service has **no fee** and a published **10-minute** estimate. The RPTOP does not itself establish that the tax has been paid.

## Pay the assessed tax

Proceed to the **City Treasurer’s Office, ground floor, City Hall Building**, with the **RPTOP and previous year’s official property tax receipt**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

Have the tax and any arrears verified, receive the billing statement, submit it at the payment counter, and pay the assessed amount. Keep the official receipt. The charter mentions a fast lane for senior citizens, PWDs, and pregnant women where applicable; confirm the current counter arrangements onsite.

## Published rates and timing

The saved charter lists **2% basic tax plus 1% Special Education Fund**, and delinquency interest of **2% per month, up to 36 months or 72%**. Obtain the current computation and applicable assessment basis, reliefs, and payment dates from the Treasurer. The document does not provide a complete current deadline or discount schedule.

Published payment estimate: **30 minutes without arrears**, **50 minutes with arrears**. The RPTOP and payment charters show different counter numbers; ask the assistance desk for current routing.
