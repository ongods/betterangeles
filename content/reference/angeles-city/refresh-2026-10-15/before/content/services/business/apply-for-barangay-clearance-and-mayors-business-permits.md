# Apply for barangay clearance and mayor’s business permits — Angeles City

Prepare a new business permit application for the Angeles City Business Permit and Licensing Division.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to apply

**Business Permit and Licensing Division (BPLD), ground floor, City Hall Building**, through the Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS). Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## New business requirements

- Completed Business Permit Application Form.
- Proof of business registration or legal personality: DTI, SEC, or CDA, as applicable.
- Business capitalization as the basis for computing taxes, fees, and charges.
- Occupancy permit for a newly constructed building.
- Barangay clearance; the application form specifies this when an occupancy permit is not required.
- Lease contract if you rent the premises.
- Community Tax Certificate (cedula).

Download the saved [Business Permit Application Form](/services/business/download-business-permit-application-form) and complete the **New** section. The saved form is revision 30, dated September 1, 2023; ask BPLD whether a newer form is required.

The form uses business capitalization as the tax basis and makes barangay clearance conditional on the occupancy-permit requirement. The separate charter lists barangay clearance in its new-business checklist and gives income tax returns as an example of tax-basis evidence. Confirm BPLD’s applicable checklist rather than assuming that the form’s shorter list waives another requirement.

For barangay clearance, use the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay) to locate the barangay where the business operates. The scraped directory does not specify clearance fees or application requirements; confirm these with that barangay.

## Application steps

1. Submit your application and requirements at BPLD/BOSS for checking.
2. Wait for assessment of declared capitalization or gross receipts. New applications are routed for site inspection and regulatory clearances.
3. Proceed to the City Treasurer’s verification and assessment desk, then pay the assessed city fees and keep the official receipt.
4. Pay the separately assessed Fire Safety Inspection Certificate fee at the Bureau of Fire Protection collecting desk.
5. Complete zoning, fire, and sanitary clearance checks as directed.
6. Claim the validated business permit and sticker after approval.

## Fees and processing

The charter bases city fees on the **Angeles City Tax Ordinance** and the declared capital or gross receipts, with a separate BFP assessment. It publishes **two days for new applications, subject to inspection**. Obtain the actual assessment from BPLD and the Treasurer rather than calculating a fixed total from this guide.
