# Renew permits and pay local business taxes — Angeles City

Use the Angeles City renewal checklist and the published BPLD/BOSS assessment procedure.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to apply

**BPLD/BOSS, ground floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Renewal requirements

The saved application form lists:

- Completed application marked **Renewal**.
- Financial statements or income tax return as the basis for taxes, fees, and charges.
- Barangay clearance.
- Community Tax Certificate (cedula).

Declare gross sales or receipts in the business activity section. The form instructs renewal applicants to complete the other-information section only when information has changed. It offers annual, semi-annual, and quarterly payment choices; confirm which arrangement BPLD permits for your account.

## Renewal steps

1. Submit your application and requirements at BPLD/BOSS for checking.
2. Wait at the Taxpayer’s Lounge while staff assess the declared gross sales or receipts and process the renewal. Confirm any inspection or regulatory clearance needed for your application.
3. Proceed to the City Treasurer’s verification and assessment desk, then pay the assessed city fees and keep the official receipt.
4. Pay the separately assessed Fire Safety Inspection Certificate fee at the Bureau of Fire Protection collecting desk.
5. Complete zoning, fire, and sanitary clearance checks as directed.
6. Claim the validated business permit and sticker after approval.

## Fees, timing, and deadlines

The charter publishes **one day for renewal without inspection**. Fees depend on the city’s tax ordinance and the approved assessment. The saved sources do not give a current annual filing deadline, renewal venue changes, online payment instructions, or a complete penalty schedule. Ask BPLD for the current renewal advisory and the Treasurer for any outstanding balance.

Download the [business permit form](/services/business/download-business-permit-application-form). For a separate property-tax transaction, see [real property tax payment](/services/taxes-fees/pay-real-property-tax).
