# Apply for a Mayor’s permit for public transport — Angeles City

Prepare the local permit requirements for tricycles for hire, public utility jeepneys, school services, and mini-buses.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to apply

**Public Transportation and Regulatory Office, City Hall Compound**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Requirements

**Public utility jeepney, school service, or mini-bus:** original certification from the JODA president; photocopies of vehicle Official Receipt (OR) and Certificate of Registration (CR); photocopy of the LTFRB decision.

**Tricycle for hire:** original certification from the TODA president; original barangay clearance as proof of residency; photocopies of OR and CR, cedula, and valid ID or driver’s license. If the operator does not live in the barangay where the tricycle will operate, the charter also lists an original barangay certification.

## Steps

1. Submit the requirements and obtain an order of payment.
2. Pay at the City Treasurer’s Office and make a photocopy of the official receipt as instructed.
3. Present the receipt to the processing officer for permit preparation.
4. Have the vehicle checked for body number, panel route, and color scheme.
5. After approval, receive the Mayor’s permit and sticker.
