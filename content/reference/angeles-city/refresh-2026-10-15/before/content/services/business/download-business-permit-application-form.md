Access the saved Angeles City application form for a new permit, renewal, or amendment.

Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Form version

The saved form is **BPLD-ABP-001, revision 30, September 1, 2023**. Confirm the accepted version with BPLD before filing. The downloadable copy below retains the original form PDF.

## Information to prepare

- Application type: new, renewal, or amendment; requested payment mode.
- TIN and DTI, SEC, or CDA registration number; business type.
- Owner’s name, business and trade names, business and owner addresses, and contact details.
- Emergency contact, business area, employee counts, and CCTV count.
- Lessor information and monthly rent if the premises are rented.
- Business activities, units, delivery trucks, capitalization for a new business, or gross sales/receipts for renewal.
- Signature, position or title, and a sketch of the business location.

Complete the form accurately and legibly. The form says incomplete applications will be returned. It directs online applicants to e-BOSS through the [Angeles City Government website](https://www.angelescity.gov.ph/); confirm the current filing link with BPLD.

See the [new business checklist](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits) or [renewal checklist](/services/business/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes) before submitting.
