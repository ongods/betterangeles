# Access educational support programs from the LGU — Angeles City

Find the documented educational assistance assessment and public school information for Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Educational financial assistance

The CSWDO Citizen’s Charter lists an educational assistance assessment for individuals and families needing help. Prepare a **personal request letter, enrollment form or certification of current enrollment, barangay certificate of indigency, valid ID, and student school ID**.

Go to **CSWDO, City Hall Compound** for an initial interview, document review, and home-visit validation. The saved charter lists **no application fee** and assessment **within five days**. Approval and the claiming schedule are communicated after review; the document does not give a fixed award amount. Read the full [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance) guide.

## School information and program updates

Use the [Angeles City public school directory](/services/education/find-public-schools-in-angeles-city) to identify schools listed in the DepEd Angeles City dashboard. That dataset records infrastructure-project submissions; it does not describe enrollment vacancies, scholarships, or club schedules.

The city’s [July 15, 2025 education announcement](https://www.angelescity.gov.ph/view_news?newsID=8bcaf22d8fccf535f5cd0d2df7ab722c0700) describes a review of DepEd priorities and a proposal to improve student computer access. It is a historical policy announcement, not an open application or a confirmed distribution schedule. Ask your school about programs currently available.
