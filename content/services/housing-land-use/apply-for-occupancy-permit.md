# Apply for an occupancy permit — Angeles City

Prepare the completion documents and inspections for an Angeles City occupancy permit.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

> **City Hall schedule update (October 15, 2026):** The city now publishes a Monday–Thursday compressed workweek and a dated October 19–22 adjustment. Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to go

City Engineer’s Office, **3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Requirements

- Completed occupancy permit forms: certificate of completion, certificate of supervision, and endorsement form.
- Approved building plans and building permit.
- Completed construction logbook.

## Steps and processing

1. Submit the application and documents for verification.
2. Complete the zoning and BFP fire-safety inspection endorsements.
3. Have the constructed building inspected and its conformity with approved plans reviewed.
4. Obtain the assessment and pay the fees at the City Treasurer’s Office.
5. Claim the occupancy permit after final review and approval.

The charter lists fees based on the National Building Code schedule and **four hours and 25 minutes** of processing. Actual release depends on zoning approval and issuance of the BFP Fire Safety Inspection Certificate.
