# Request zoning, land use, or building permits — Angeles City

Prepare the construction zoning clearance and building permit documents published by Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

> **City Hall schedule update (October 15, 2026):** The city now publishes a Monday–Thursday compressed workweek and a dated October 19–22 adjustment. Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to go

City Engineer’s Office, **3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Building permit requirements

The charter covers anyone seeking to build or improve a building in Angeles City. Prepare:

- Certified true copy of the Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT).
- Lease contract, deed of sale, or landowner’s consent, as applicable.
- Real property tax declaration and real property tax receipt.
- Certified location plan.
- Completed building, sanitary/plumbing, electrical/electronics, and mechanical permit forms.
- Certified architectural, structural, sanitary, electrical/electronics, and mechanical building plans.
- Bill of materials and specifications.
- Barangay clearance for the building location.
- Building owner’s TIN and Community Tax Certificate (cedula).
- A 2 ft × 3 ft signboard.

The charter additionally lists structural analysis for buildings of two stories or more; soil testing and a fire-protection plan for three stories or more; a 3-inch standpipe plan for three- and four-story buildings, with a sprinkler system optional; and sprinkler-system and sewerage-treatment plans for five stories or more. Confirm the plans applicable to your project with the building official.

## Construction zoning compliance

This is a separate review associated with the building permit. The **City Planning and Development Office, 3rd floor, City Hall Building**, checks the application endorsed by the Office of the City Building Official.

Its basic checklist lists: application form; certified TCT and deed of sale, contract to sell, or lease/owner consent; real property tax declaration; latest property tax receipt or tax clearance; location plan; building and architectural plans; bill of materials; specifications; and barangay clearance.

Additional items depend on the project: structural, sanitary/plumbing, and electrical plans; neighborhood consent for manufacturing in an R3 residential zone; homeowners’ consent in R1/R2 subdivisions; DENR ECC/EIA and Civil Aviation Authority clearance for specified buildings, sites, and uses; DOH clearance for cell sites and hospitals; and the listed Sangguniang resolution for the applicable projects. Ask the zoning office to identify the relevant items from the full charter.

## Application steps

1. Submit the completed building application and supporting documents to the City Engineer’s Office.
2. Arrange the site inspection and complete the planning/zoning and BFP fire-safety review.
3. Follow up after the technical plans are reviewed and obtain the assessed order of payment.
4. Pay at the City Treasurer’s Office and keep the official receipt.
5. Claim the permit after final review and approval.

## Published fees and timing

Building fees follow the schedule under the National Building Code. The building charter estimates **four hours and 35 minutes of processing**, with actual release dependent on locational and fire-safety clearances. The construction-zoning charter estimates **one day, two hours, and 25 minutes for approved applications**, with fees varying by construction type and project cost; its complete fee table is in the saved PDF. These are separate estimates.

For a business location rather than construction, see [business zoning compliance](/services/business/request-business-zoning-compliance). After construction, see [occupancy permits](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-occupancy-permit).
