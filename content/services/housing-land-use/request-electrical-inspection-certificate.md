## Where to go

City Engineer’s Office, **3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Requirements and procedure

The charter covers people applying for an electrical connection **without a certificate of occupancy**. It lists a **site sketch or map** and a **completed Permit for Temporary Service Connection form signed by a Registered Electrical Engineer**.

Submit the requirements, confirm the inspection schedule, and have the installation inspected. Obtain the assessed order of payment, pay at the City Treasurer’s Office, and follow up for technical review, approval, and certificate release.

Fees follow the National Building Code and National Electrical Code schedules. The saved charter estimates **three hours of processing** and **release within two working days**. The business-page version includes the detailed procedure; both publications are linked below.
