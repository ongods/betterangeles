# Request an annual inspection certificate — Angeles City

Apply for the annual inspection certificate for a commercial establishment in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

> **City Hall schedule update (October 15, 2026):** The city now publishes a Monday–Thursday compressed workweek and a dated October 19–22 adjustment. Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to go

City Engineer’s Office, **3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Requirements

For renewal, the charter lists **either a photocopy of the certificate of occupancy or the previous annual inspection certificate**. For a new application, it lists a **site sketch**, with a certificate of occupancy optional.

## Steps, charges, and timing

Submit the requirements, arrange the site inspection, obtain the assessed order of payment, and pay at the City Treasurer’s Office. Present the receipt to the City Building Official’s office for technical verification, approval, and release.

Fees follow the National Building Code schedule. The saved estimate is **three hours and 30 minutes of actual processing**, with **release within two working days** under normal conditions.
