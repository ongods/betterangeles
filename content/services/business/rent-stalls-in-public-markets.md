# Rent stalls in public markets — Angeles City

Ask about market stall availability and rental requirements in Angeles City.

## Where to start

Ask the city or the barangay hosting the market which office manages the stalls. Confirm vacancies, allocation rules, rental charges, deposits, and the application checklist before paying. The supplied Angeles sources do not contain a public-market rental procedure or fee schedule.

Use the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay) for an official local starting point. A stall allocation and a business permit are separate matters; see the [business permit guide](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits) for the published business requirements.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
