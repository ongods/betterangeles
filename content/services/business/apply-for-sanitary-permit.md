# Apply for a sanitary permit — Angeles City

Follow the sanitation permit procedure included in Angeles City’s business permit process.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

> **City Hall schedule update (October 15, 2026):** The city now publishes a Monday–Thursday compressed workweek and a dated October 19–22 adjustment. Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to apply

Start with the business permit application at **BPLD/BOSS**. The **City Health Office, 3rd floor, City Hall Building**, handles sanitation review. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Requirements and steps

The charter lists a **completed Business Permit Application Form**, available at BPLD/BOSS, for business establishment owners.

1. Submit the business permit application and receive the BOSS endorsement for sanitation review.
2. Wait while the environmental health processing clerk verifies the application.
3. If inspection is required, confirm the inspection date and meet the sanitary inspector at the premises. Correct any adverse findings.
4. Follow up at BPLD for approval. The sanitary permit is prepared for release with the business permit.

## Published fee and processing time

The saved charter lists the sanitary fee as **50% of the Mayor’s Permit fee**, included in the computation and payment of the business permit. Its estimates are **five minutes without inspection** and **one day and 15 minutes with inspection**. Confirm the assessment with BOSS; this is not a second instruction to pay an already included fee.

Worker health cards have a separate procedure: [worker health certificate cards](/services/health-services/apply-for-worker-health-certificate-cards).
