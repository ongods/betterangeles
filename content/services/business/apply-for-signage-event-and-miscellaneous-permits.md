# Apply for signage, event, and miscellaneous permits — Angeles City

Find the published permit requirements for advertising displays, motorcades, performances, and other activities in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Basic requirements

All four saved guidelines list a **completed application form** and a **request letter addressed to the City Mayor**. BPLD evaluates the documents and routes them for approval.

## Requirements by activity

| Activity                                                   | Additional documents or information                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Billboards, signboards, advertisements, banners, streamers | Content, size, and material specifications; objective and intended display locations. Site and structure approval is routed to the Office of the City Building Official.                                                         |
| Motorcade                                                  | Route or itinerary and time. The Traffic Enforcement and Transport Management Office checks conflicts; rescheduling or rerouting may be required.                                                                                |
| Religious, cultural, civic, or government activity         | The saved guideline lists no additional requirements beyond the basic checklist.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Transport of property                                      | Barangay clearance.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Carnival or circus                                         | Details including number of contrivances and activity duration; barangay resolution for the venue; lease contract.                                                                                                               |
| Cockfight                                                  | Resolution from the barangay where the event will be held.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Boxing match                                               | Games and Amusements Board clearance and neighborhood consent from the barangay where the event will be held.                                                                                                                    |
| Victory party                                              | Statement of objective and statement of activity.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Variety show or concert                                    | Statement of objective; nature and schedule of activity; a neighborhood or administrator requirement for public places, whose wording should be confirmed with BPLD. Public-place applications are routed for zoning conformity. |

## Steps, fees, and processing

The variety-show guideline spells the neighborhood or administrator requirement as **“Concert”**. Its intended wording is unclear; ask BPLD whether a consent document is required and which document it accepts.

1. Submit the application and supporting documents to BPLD.
2. Complete any additional requirements and routing for the Mayor’s approval and the relevant site, traffic, or zoning review.
3. Receive an order of payment and pay the assessed fees at the City Treasurer’s Office.
4. Receive the permit for final approval and signature as directed, and provide a file copy to BPLD.

Each saved guideline lists **20–25 minutes** and fees based on the **Angeles City Tax Ordinance**. This processing estimate does not guarantee that separate clearances or corrections will be completed in the same visit.
