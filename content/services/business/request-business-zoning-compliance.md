# Request zoning compliance for a business permit — Angeles City

Check the published location and zoning requirements for an Angeles City business permit.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Office and eligibility

**City Planning and Development Office, 3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter. The charter covers business groups and people engaged in business and makes this clearance part of the BOSS process.

## Requirements

- Sketch of the business location.
- Homeowners’ approval for R1 and R2 subdivisions, where applicable.
- Neighborhood consent or barangay resolution, where applicable.
- Environmental impact assessment for junkshops and manufacturing.
- Occupancy permit for businesses occupying new buildings.

## Process and published charges

The zoning office evaluates the application endorsed through BOSS. If inspection is required, meet the inspector at the site and follow up on the report. The office issues a certificate for an approved application or a denial letter for a disapproved one; follow any instructions for re-inspection.

The charter lists **₱180, included in the business permit payment**. It estimates **10 minutes without inspection**, or **one day, one hour, and 15 minutes with inspection**.

For construction rather than a business clearance, use [building and construction zoning permits](/services/housing-land-use/request-zoning-land-use-or-building-permits). The source PDF below is headed **Zoning Compliance for Business Permit**, even though the scraped record labels it as a building-permit service.
