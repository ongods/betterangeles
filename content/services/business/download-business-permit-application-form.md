# Download the business permit application form — Angeles City

Access the saved Angeles City application form for a new permit, renewal, or amendment.

> Updated October 15, 2026. The city homepage now links to revision 33 of the business permit application form.

## Form version

Download **BPLD-ABP-001, revision 33, dated October 1, 2025**: [current form PDF](/documents/angeles-updates-2026-10-15/BPLD-ABP-001-Rev-33.pdf) · [official publication](https://angelescity.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BPLD-ABP-001-Rev-33.pdf).

The October 2026 upload path is not the form's revision date. Compared with the August saved form, revision 33 adds **BFP certification** to both the new-business and renewal checklists. It also asks whether the applicant enjoys tax incentives from a government entity.

The previous form, **revision 30, dated September 1, 2023**, remains available as an [archived copy](/documents/angeles-services/001-business-permit-form.pdf).

## Information to prepare

- Application type: new, renewal, or amendment; requested payment mode.
- TIN and DTI, SEC, or CDA registration number; business type.
- Owner’s name, business and trade names, business and owner addresses, and contact details.
- Emergency contact, business area, employee counts, and CCTV count.
- Lessor information and monthly rent if the premises are rented.
- Business activities, units, delivery trucks, capitalization for a new business, or gross sales/receipts for renewal.
- Signature, position or title, and a sketch of the business location.

Complete the form accurately and legibly. The form says incomplete applications will be returned. For online filing, the [city homepage](https://angelescity.gov.ph/) now links directly to [eBPLS](https://cityofangeles.ph/bpls/).

See the [new business checklist](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits) or [renewal checklist](/services/business/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes) before submitting.
