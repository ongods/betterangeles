# Access maternal care and child immunization — Angeles City

Find the published maternal care and immunization procedures for Angeles City health centers.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Maternal care

The charter covers **pregnant and lactating mothers**, with services every **Thursday** at RHUs and BHSs. It lists no service fee and a **30-minute** processing estimate. Bring your **Home-based Maternal Record (HBMR)**. New patients provide the information needed for staff to create the record.

1. Present your HBMR or register as a new patient.
2. Have your vital signs taken for risk assessment.
3. Receive the physical assessment and any screening requests from health staff.
4. Receive guidance on maternal nutrition and care, and confirm your follow-up visit.

## Child and maternal immunization

The saved immunization charter covers infants **0–12 months old** and **second-trimester pregnant mothers**. It lists RHU services on **Wednesdays, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break**, with **no fee** and a **25-minute** estimate.

- Returning infants: bring the ECCD card.
- Returning mothers: bring the home-based maternal record.
- New patients: provide personal information; staff create the appropriate record.
- Staff check the record and vital signs before immunization. Ask them which vaccines and follow-up dates apply under the current program.

## Office

The charter identifies the City Health Office at the **3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Services above are delivered through RHUs and BHSs; confirm the nearest clinic through your barangay using the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay).
