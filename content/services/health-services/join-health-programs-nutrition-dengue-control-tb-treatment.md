# Join health programs (nutrition, dengue control, TB treatment) — Angeles City

Ask Angeles City health staff about current nutrition, dengue-control, and TB program access.

## Where to start

Ask your RHU or barangay health station which programs are currently available, who can participate, and what assessment or referral is needed. The supplied service documents describe consultation, maternal care, and immunization; they do not contain dedicated nutrition, dengue-control, or TB-treatment enrollment rules.

Start with the [published consultation procedure](/services/health-services/get-free-check-ups-basic-medicines-and-vaccines) or find your barangay through the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay).

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
