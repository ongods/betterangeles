# Go to the local hospital for treatment or confinement — Angeles City

Find the documented Angeles City assessment and referral routes for medical assistance.

## Where to start

For a health-center assessment, see [consultation services](/services/health-services/get-free-check-ups-basic-medicines-and-vaccines). The saved charter directs cases with complications to a public health nurse or rural health physician.

For help with hospital costs, see [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance) or [social case study reports and referrals](/services/social-welfare/get-family-and-community-welfare-support). The referral charter names AUF, JBL, PCSO, and other institutions as recipients; it does not publish hospital admission rules, bed availability, emergency telephone numbers, or a hospital directory. Confirm admission requirements and charges directly with the receiving hospital.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
