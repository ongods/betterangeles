# Attend pre-marriage counseling — Angeles City

Arrange the published pre-marriage counseling and responsible parenthood seminar for Angeles City applicants.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Eligibility, requirement, and schedule

The counseling charter covers couples about to marry aged **18–25**. Bring the **Notice of Application of Marriage License** from the Local Civil Registrar. The saved schedule is **Friday, 8:00 a.m.–12:00 noon**, with a **three-hour** processing estimate and **no fee**.

## Registration and attendance

1. After applying at the Local Civil Registrar, present the notice at the City Health Office information desk.
2. Complete the information sheet and marriage-expectation questionnaire. Staff advise you when to return.
3. The City Health Office forwards the questionnaire to CSWDO before the seminar.
4. Attend the counseling session, conducted together with the City Health Office responsible parenthood seminar.
5. Receive the counseling certificate and submit it to the Local Civil Registrar.

CSWDO is listed at **City Hall Compound**, and the City Health Office at **3rd floor, City Hall Building**. Confirm the current seminar venue when registering. The marriage-license checklist separately specifies counseling for ages 18–24; ask the registrar which age-specific documents apply to each partner. See [marriage license requirements](/services/civil-documents/apply-for-marriage-license).
