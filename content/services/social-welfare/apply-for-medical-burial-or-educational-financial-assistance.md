# Apply for medical, burial, or educational financial assistance — Angeles City

Request a CSWDO assessment for medical, burial, or educational assistance in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), **City Hall Compound**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Who can apply?

The charter covers individuals and families needing medical, burial, or educational assistance. It describes an **assessment**, with approval or denial after review. It does not publish a guaranteed payment amount.

## Documents to prepare

For all three types: **personal request letter, barangay certificate of indigency, and valid identification card**.

| Assistance  | Additional requirements                                                                              |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Medical     | Medical certificate or latest medical abstract.                                                      |
| Burial      | Death certificate and funeral contract.                                                              |
| Educational | Copy of enrollment form or school certification of current enrollment, plus the student’s school ID. |

## Assessment procedure

1. Explain your need during the initial interview; staff give you the applicable requirements.
2. Submit the documents for eligibility review and receive the home-visit schedule.
3. Allow the social worker to validate the information and prepare the assessment or social case study report.
4. Wait for approval. Approved applicants are notified by call or text about the claiming schedule; disapproved applicants receive a formal notice.

## Fee and timing

The saved charter lists **no fee** and an assessment timeframe **within five days** under normal circumstances. This is not a promise of payment within five days. For assistance from another institution, see [social case study reports and referral letters](/services/social-welfare/get-family-and-community-welfare-support).
