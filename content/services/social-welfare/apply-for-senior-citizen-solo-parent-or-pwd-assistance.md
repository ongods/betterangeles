## Senior citizen ID eligibility

The saved charter lists **age 60 or above**, **Angeles City residency**, and **Filipino citizenship, including dual citizenship**. It states a **six-month city-residency condition** alongside the citizenship requirement; confirm how OSCA applies that condition to your application.

## Requirements

- Photocopy of one valid identification card.
- Barangay clearance.
- One 2×2 photograph and two 1×1 photographs.
- Fully completed application form with a clear signature.

## Where to apply and steps

**Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA), ground floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

Submit the completed form and documents. Staff review and encode the information, prepare the card using your photo and signature, and release the ID and booklet. The saved charter lists **no fee** and **30 minutes** under normal conditions.

## Solo parent and PWD assistance

The reviewed sources do not include a complete solo-parent or PWD application checklist, ongoing benefits schedule, or dedicated office contacts. Ask **CSWDO at the City Hall Compound** for the current program and referral instructions. For medical, burial, or educational needs, see [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance); eligibility is assessed individually.
