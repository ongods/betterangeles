# Request a property assessment certification — Angeles City

Prepare a request for a property assessment certification from the Angeles City Assessor.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

> **City Hall schedule update (October 15, 2026):** The city now publishes a Monday–Thursday compressed workweek and a dated October 19–22 adjustment. Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to go

City Assessor’s Office, **ground floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Eligibility and requirements

The charter covers property owners, authorized representatives, and people with a legal interest in the property. Bring the **tax declaration or property owner’s name**, plus a **request slip from a government agency or a person with a legal interest in the property**.

## Steps, fee, and time

Submit the requirements so staff can verify the database record. If available, obtain an order of payment, pay at the City Treasurer’s Office, and return with the receipt to claim the approved certification.

Published fee: **₱50 per certification**. Published estimate: **20 minutes**. The charter explicitly **excludes a Certificate of No Improvements** from this service; ask the Assessor about the separate procedure if that document is needed.
