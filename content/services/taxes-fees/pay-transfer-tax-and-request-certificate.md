# Pay transfer tax and request a transfer tax certificate — Angeles City

Prepare the documents listed for a property transfer tax assessment and certificate in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to go

City Treasurer’s Office, **ground floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Documents to prepare

The charter covers people or corporations acquiring registered property in Angeles City through conveyance. It lists:

- Copy of the conveyance document, such as deed of sale, donation, extrajudicial settlement, assignment, consolidation affidavit, or certificate of sale.
- Certified true copies of tax declarations for land and any building or improvements.
- Certificate of No Improvement if applicable, or a certificate of existing improvements covering registered owners where required.
- Copy of the certificate of tax clearance.
- One documentary stamp.

## Steps and published charges

Submit the requirements for verification and tax computation. Pay the assessed amount and retain the receipt. Staff prepare the certificate, verify it through the land-tax division, and obtain the Treasurer’s signature before release.

Both saved publications describe this same transaction: **₱50 for the transfer tax certificate**, plus **0.75% transfer tax** on the applicable property value in the conveyance document, assessed value, or fair market value. Ask the Treasurer to determine the applicable basis and current amount. Published processing estimate: **one hour**.

See [certified tax declarations](/services/taxes-fees/request-certified-tax-declaration) for one of the supporting documents. The two source records are consolidated here to avoid presenting certificate issuance as a second independent payment.
