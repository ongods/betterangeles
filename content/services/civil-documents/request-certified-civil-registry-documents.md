# Request certified civil registry documents — Angeles City

Request certified local copies of birth, marriage, death, or other records registered in Angeles City.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

> **City Hall schedule update (October 15, 2026):** The city now publishes a Monday–Thursday compressed workweek and a dated October 19–22 adjustment. Check the [office schedule advisory](/government/departments/office-schedules) before visiting. Any Monday–Friday hours quoted below are from the August charter; confirm clinic and essential-service schedules separately.

## Where to go

Local Civil Registry, **2nd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## Eligibility and requirements

The charter covers records registered in the city and requests by **document owners or their authorized representatives**. Bring a **completed request slip**, available at the registrar’s window, or a photocopy of the document, plus a **valid identification card**. Ask the office what proof of authorization is required for a representative.

## Steps

1. Submit the request so staff can search the database or registry book.
2. If the record is found, obtain an order of payment and pay at the City Treasurer’s Office.
3. Return with the receipt while staff prepare and certify the copy.
4. Collect the reviewed and signed document. If no record is found, staff advise whether delayed registration is appropriate.

The saved charter lists **₱50 per certified transcription/true copy** or **₱50 per certified photocopy of the original**, with **20 minutes** of processing under normal conditions. This is the local registry’s certified-copy service; ask the receiving institution whether it specifically requires a PSA-issued document.
