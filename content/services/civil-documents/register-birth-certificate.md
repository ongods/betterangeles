# Register a birth certificate — Angeles City

Find the on-time and delayed birth registration requirements published by the Angeles City Local Civil Registry.

> Based on Angeles City Government documents retrieved on August 11, 2026. Fees, office locations, schedules, and processing times below are those published in the saved documents. Confirm current arrangements with the office before visiting; processing estimates assume complete requirements and normal conditions.

## Where to go

Local Civil Registry, **2nd floor, City Hall Building**. Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., without a noon break, as listed in the saved charter.

## On-time registration

The saved charter says a newborn should be registered **within 30 days of birth** by the hospital representative, birth attendant, or parents. Submit the **completed Certificate of Live Birth signed by the physician or birth attendant**.

For unmarried parents, the charter says both parents should appear and additionally lists an **affidavit of acknowledgment/admission of paternity** and an **affidavit to use the father’s surname (RA 9255)**. Ask the registrar which documents apply to your circumstances.

Published fee: **none for the usual registration of married or single parents**; **₱460 for the unmarried-parent procedure**, broken down as ₱300 for the surname affidavit, three ₱50 certifications, and ₱10 UPLC. Published time: **10 minutes usual registration**, **30 minutes unmarried-parent procedure**.

The saved fee panel uses both “single parents” and “unmarried parents” without explaining the distinction. Confirm the applicable procedure and fee with the registrar.

## Delayed registration

The charter lists a **completed and signed Certificate of Live Birth** and a mandatory **PSA Certificate of No Registration**, plus age-specific evidence:

| Age             | Supporting evidence listed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1–11 months     | Medical certificate from the hospital or birth attendant; immunization card.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 1–7 years       | Baptismal certificate and medical certificate.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 8–17 years      | Baptismal certificate and Form 137 school record.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 18–59 years     | Any two listed documents, with at least one old document: baptismal certificate, voter’s certification, Form 137, residence certificate/cedula, passport, police or NBI clearance, SSS E-1, PhilHealth MDR, or marriage certificate for a married applicant. |
| Senior citizens | Any two listed documents: senior citizen application form, postal ID, SSS E-1, old ID, residence certificate/cedula, or affidavit of two disinterested persons.                                                                                              |

The unmarried-parent affidavits are also listed for delayed registration. Published fee: **₱250 usual delayed registration**, **₱710 for the unmarried-parent procedure**. Published timing: **25 minutes processing, a 10-day posting period, and five minutes releasing**.

## Steps

Submit documents for review and interview. Pay only after the registry issues an order of payment, then return with the official receipt. For on-time registration, complete the registrar’s signature and registry-number steps. For delayed registration, return after the required posting period to collect the registered document.
