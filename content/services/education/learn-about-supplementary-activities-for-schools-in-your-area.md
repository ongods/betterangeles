# Learn about supplementary activities for schools in your area — Angeles City

Use Angeles City school records to find the right school for program inquiries.

## Where to start

Use the [Angeles City public school directory](/services/education/find-public-schools-in-angeles-city) to identify a school, then ask its administration about current clubs, tutorials, sports, enrichment activities, eligibility, costs, and consent requirements.

The supplied DepEd dataset records school IDs and infrastructure-project submission information. A project count does not mean the school offers that many extracurricular activities. The city’s [July 15, 2025 education announcement](https://www.angelescity.gov.ph/view_news?newsID=8bcaf22d8fccf535f5cd0d2df7ab722c0700) discusses proposed educational priorities, rather than a published activity timetable.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
