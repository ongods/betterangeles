# Apply for local scholarships — Angeles City

Find where to confirm scholarship calls and educational assistance in Angeles City.

## Where to start

Ask your school and check [Angeles City announcements](https://www.angelescity.gov.ph/news) for current scholarship calls. Confirm the program name, eligibility, required grades and documents, award amount, and application deadline with the issuing office. The supplied sources do not include a scholarship application or an active scholarship schedule.

For the documented CSWDO educational financial assistance assessment, see [financial assistance assessment](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-medical-burial-or-educational-financial-assistance). Its document checklist includes proof of enrollment and the student’s school ID. Financial assistance assessment is a different process from a scholarship application.

Use the [Angeles City public school directory](/services/education/find-public-schools-in-angeles-city) to identify schools listed in the supplied DepEd data.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
