# Enroll children in LGU daycare or preschool programs — Angeles City

Find where to ask about daycare or preschool enrollment in Angeles City.

## Where to start

Ask your barangay hall which child development center serves your address and confirm the enrollment period, age requirements, documents, fees, and available places. The supplied records do not include a daycare directory or an Angeles enrollment checklist.

Use the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay) to find your official barangay page. The [Angeles City public school directory](/services/education/find-public-schools-in-angeles-city) contains DepEd school records and does not establish which centers offer LGU daycare or preschool.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
