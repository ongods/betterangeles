# Request special waste collection for hazardous materials and electronics — Angeles City

Confirm approved collection or drop-off options for special waste in Angeles City.

## Where to start

Ask your barangay about the designated collection or disposal channel for your specific item before placing it with routine household waste. Explain the item type, quantity, and whether it contains batteries, chemicals, or other special materials. Use the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay) to find the official local page.

The supplied sources do not include an Angeles hazardous-waste or e-waste pickup program, accredited collector list, drop-off address, or charge schedule. Obtain the appropriate handling and disposal instructions from the receiving office or collector.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
