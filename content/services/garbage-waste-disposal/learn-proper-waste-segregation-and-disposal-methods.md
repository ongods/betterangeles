# Learn proper waste segregation and disposal methods — Angeles City

Ask your Angeles City barangay for its published waste-sorting and disposal instructions.

## Where to start

Ask your barangay which waste categories it collects, how each should be separated and packaged, and where to bring items excluded from regular collection. Use the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay) for official local page links.

The supplied Angeles sources do not contain a local segregation checklist, materials recovery facility directory, or disposal-site instructions. Follow the barangay’s current published guidance for your address.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
