# Check garbage collection schedules and request pickup — Angeles City

Confirm the collection schedule for your address with your Angeles City barangay.

## Where to start

Use the [Angeles City barangay directory](/services/business/find-your-angeles-city-barangay) to locate the official page for your barangay. Ask the barangay hall about collection days, accepted waste, set-out times, missed pickups, and whether a special pickup can be arranged. Provide your address and the waste type and quantity when making an inquiry.

The supplied directory identifies 33 barangays; it does not publish garbage truck routes, pickup frequency, collection fees, or a citywide schedule. Confirm these details locally before setting waste out.

## Source scope

Directory references on this page use official city or DepEd records captured in August 2026. Confirm current service arrangements with the responsible office.
